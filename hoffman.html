THE ROLE

Intern Project
Engineer

I joined Hoffman Construction's project team as an MEP-focused Project Engineer Intern on the EPIC Center owned by Applied Materials. Hoffman served as the project's general contractor. My day-to-day work centered on communicating technical information clearly, maintaining coordination across subcontractors, and helping the team keep field activity aligned with current project requirements.

The project was well underway and entering commissioning during my internship. That context made accuracy, follow-through, and visibility into outstanding work especially important to supporting project completion.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES

Technical coordination

Prepared, distributed, and tracked technical RFIs, from confirming conditions observed in the field to resolving questions around intended design specifications. I also prepared meeting minutes for weekly subcontractor coordination.

Meeting facilitation

Supported recurring subcontractor coordination meetings organized around a defined agenda. Discussions addressed safety, commissioning, RFIs, submittals, and key action items so that open issues and responsibilities remained visible.

Schedule awareness

Monitored field progress against the published schedule and helped surface work that could affect planned sequencing or milestone dates.

Design verification walks

Participated in design-verification walks with the architect, comparing specifications, tolerances, and available data in the field against the most current drawings and plans. The goal was to identify missed details before they could lead to costly change orders or redesign.

Commissioning support

As commissioning became a central focus, I used Smart Completions to track equipment deliverables, testing, verification data, and construction items that could prevent systems from receiving signoff as complete.

Closeout readiness

By maintaining visibility into incomplete deliverables and tests, I helped the team understand the remaining work required to support on-time system signoff.
